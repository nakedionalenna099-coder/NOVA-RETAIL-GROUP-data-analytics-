Nova Retail Group Data Analytics Project This project focuses on analysing sales data for Nova Retail Group to understand business performance, identify trends, and support data-driven decision-making. ### Project Objectives - Analyse sales revenue and profit. - Identify monthly revenue trends. - Compare revenue across product categories and regions. - Explore customer and store data. - Identify opportunities for business improvement. ### Tools and Skills - SQL: Querying and analysing data. - Power BI: Creating dashboards and visualising key performance indicators. - Python: exploring and analysing data using pandas. - Data cleaning: checking data quality, handling inconsistencies, and preparing data for analysis. - Data ethics: Understanding responsible data use, GDPR, and South Africa’s POPIA.
